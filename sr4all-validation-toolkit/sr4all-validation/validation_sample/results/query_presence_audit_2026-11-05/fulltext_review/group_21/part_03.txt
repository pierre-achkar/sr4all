 strong evidence
that female gender, vitamin D deﬁciency, osteoporosis, high
TC level, migraine, and head trauma were risk factors for
the occurrence of BPPV. However, the eﬀects of other risk
factors on BPPV occurrence needed further investigations.
Further investigations should focus on exploring potential
mechanisms, how to eﬀectively intervene in high-risk
populations, and preventing these risk factors as much
as possible.
