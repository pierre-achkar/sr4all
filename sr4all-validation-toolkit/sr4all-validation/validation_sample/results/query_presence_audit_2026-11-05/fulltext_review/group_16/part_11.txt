Two studies examined the 5-HTTLPR polymorphism in 
women with BN/BSD-BPD. Traditionally, the 5-HTTLPR 
polymorphism was categorized using a biallelic model 
which included an S (short) allele and an L (long) allele, 
which coded for low or high serotonin transporter activity, 
respectively [46]. However, more recent research argues 
for a triallelic model of the 5-HTTLPR polymorphism as 
evidence suggests there is a “low-frequency” L allele vari-
ant,  LG which functions like the low-function S allele. This 
variant is simply an L allele containing an A  → G single-
nucleoid polymorphism in its sequence. Simply, the S and 
 LG alleles can be classified as “low-function” variants and 
the  LA allele can be classified as a “high-function” vari-
ant [47]. One of the studies conducted analyses using both 
the biallelic and triallelic models [20], and the other study
