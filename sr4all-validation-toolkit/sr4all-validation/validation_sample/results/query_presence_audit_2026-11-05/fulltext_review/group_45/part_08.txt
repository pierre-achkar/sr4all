Evidence Synthesis 265 
Study Selection 266 
The study selection process is illustrated in the PRISMA flow diagram (Fig 1). The 267 
initial search yielded a total of 1,198 potentially eligible titles (after removal of duplicates). 268 
Following title and abstract screening, 74 full-te xt studies were assessed for eligibility 269 
(despite multiple attempts to contact the aut hors, two reports could not be retrieved). Of 270 
these, 19 studies met the inclusion criteria. Based on the initial results, a subsequent step 271 
involved identifying 49 additional reports thr ough backward and forward citation tracking. 272 
This process resulted in the inclusion of three studies. A detailed overview of the reasons for 273 
exclusion is provided in S3 Appendix. The most common reason for exclusion was that 274 
studies did not specifically analyze the associat ion of housing insecurity and mental health 275 
among renters but rather addressed housing inse curity more broadly (e.g., statistical 276 
analyses were not separated by tenant subgroups). Overall, a total of 22 studies were 277 
included in the evidence synthesis. The interrate r reliability of the study selection process 278 
was κ  = 1, indicating almost perfect agreement (Cohen, 1960; Landis & Koch, 1977). 279 
Fig 1. PRISMA 2020 flow diagram. 280 
Study Characteristics 281 
Characteristics of the included studies are di splayed in Table 1 (housing affordability) 282 
and Table 2 (housing instability). Eleven of the studies were from the U.S., six from 283 
Australia, three from the UK, and one each from the Netherlands, South Korea, and Spain. 284 
Fourteen studies were longitudinal studies, five were cross-sectional studies, and three were 285 
quasi-experimental studies without actively manipulated interventions. The majority of the 286 
studies were secondary analyses. With the exception of one study, all were published within 287 
the last decade, mostly in 2020 or later. 288
