Reporting quality 335 
A detailed evaluation of every item of t he respective reporting guidelines for each 336 
study is provided in S4 Appendix. With the ex ception of Leifheit et al. (2021), none of the 337 
included studies explicitly stated adherence to reporting guidelines. Despite this, the 338 
included studies generally demonstrated satisfactory reporting quality, with authors 339 
transparently reporting essential information such as rationale and objectives, employed 340 
variables, analytical approaches, descriptive and analytical results, and at least some 341

that impeded accurate data extraction, methodological assessment, and potentially the 343
