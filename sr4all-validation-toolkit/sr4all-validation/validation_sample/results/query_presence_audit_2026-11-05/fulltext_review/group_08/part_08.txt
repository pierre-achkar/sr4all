About this supplement
This article has been published as part of BMC Geriatrics Volume 17
Supplement 1, 2017: The Evidence Base of Frequently prescribed drugs in older
Patients: A series of systematic reviews as a basis for recommendations in the
PRIMA-eDS-tool to reduce inappropriate polypharmacy. The full contents of the
supplement are available online at https://bmcgeriatr.biomedcentral.com/arti-
cles/supplements/volume-17-supplement-1.
