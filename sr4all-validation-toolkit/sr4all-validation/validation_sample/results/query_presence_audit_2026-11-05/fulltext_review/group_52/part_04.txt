Most of the modeling groups also published on the topic of IPV use. These publications
included IPV use for oSIAs, with some studies motivated by actual experiences (e.g., the

studies related to the role of IPV use after OPV cessation, but we did not include the

Table 2. Not surprisingly, most modeling groups published studies motivated by the global
experience with the COVID-19 pandemic, including many studies related to disruptions
