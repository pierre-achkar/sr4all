CONCLUSION
In conclusion, based on preclinical evidence, TL ’s anti-pancreatic
cancer proﬁle has been shown in diﬀerent experimental models
and under diﬀerent experimental conditions. The underlying
mechanisms were summarized, with special emphasis on
the apoptosis, autophagy, angiogenesis and related molecular
pathways. In terms of treatment allocation methods reporting,
randomization and blinding, improved study quality will
accelerate needed progress toward more clinical investigations.
