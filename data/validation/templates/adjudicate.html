How to review

  
This package is a distinct subset assigned to you. Cases with a proposed resolution appear first, followed by undecided cases. Review every card, including ones with a GPT6.1 Medium suggestion. These are post-annotation reviews, not independent first-round annotations.

  
  1. Read the extraction and the original evidence. Check the supplied PDF.
    2. 
  
  2. Compare all three judgments. Pierre's comments use the humanized wording; his labels and evidence are unchanged.
    3. 
  
  3. Choose your judgment and give a short rationale and a one-based PDF page. For a missed field, also quote its supporting text.
    4. 
  
  4. Tick the source-check box and click Save & next (or Save decision on the last case). The GPT6.1 Medium suggestion is never accepted automatically.
    5. 
  
  5. If you cannot decide, explain why and click Needs discussion. These cases go to the joint meeting. Use Next to continue without finalizing a draft.

  
Judgment rules

  
Correct: the value and its evidence are supported. Partial: generally correct, but a small part is missing or inaccurate. Incorrect: wrong or unsupported value/evidence.

  
For empty fields, choose absent (not reported in the supplied PDF) or present (reported but missed). If the source is wrong, incomplete, or ambiguous, flag discussion rather than assuming absence.

  
Judge fields independently. An objective is not automatically a research question. Do not invent exclusions by negating inclusions. Boolean operators, grouping, terms, order and database must match; ignore harmless OCR, spacing and line breaks. Reference-list or forward citation searching counts as snowballing even if it finds no extra records.

  
Two-versus-one and three-way label splits both need review. A majority is not a resolution. One assigned reviewer is not three-person consensus.

  
Autosave is only a local working copy. Export regularly and return your latest export. Import it to resume in another browser. No data are uploaded.
